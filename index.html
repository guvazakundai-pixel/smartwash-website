How To Use

Simple Steps. Brilliant Results.

Just one scoop of Smart Wash is all you need for sparkling clean clothes.

1

Measure

Measure the right amount of Smart Wash powder based on the size of your washing load. One scoop does the job.

2

Agitate

Add the powder to water and agitate to fully dissolve. Watch the rich foam build — that's the cleaning power!

3

Soak

Soak your clothes for 15–20 minutes. The foam penetrates deep into fibres, loosening dirt and stains.

4

Enjoy

Rinse and enjoy the magic! Brilliantly clean clothes with a long-lasting fresh fragrance.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Smart Wash different from other washing powders?
▼

Smart Wash features our unique rich foam formula — Inonyatsopupuma! Iyabhubhudla! — that penetrates deep into fabric fibres to lift stubborn stains. Plus, we're proudly made in Zimbabwe with local needs in mind.

Can I use Smart Wash in a washing machine?
▼

Absolutely! Smart Wash is effective for both hand washing and machine washing. For machines, simply add the recommended amount to the detergent compartment.

Where can I buy Smart Wash products?
▼

Smart Wash is available at over 500 retail outlets across Zimbabwe. You can also contact us directly for bulk or wholesale orders.

Do you deliver nationwide?
▼

Yes! We deliver across Zimbabwe. For wholesale and institutional orders, delivery is free within major cities. Contact us for delivery to your area.

How much Smart Wash should I use per wash?
▼

Just one scoop (approximately 50g) is enough for a standard wash load. For heavily soiled items, you can use a little more and soak for 15-20 minutes.

Is Smart Wash safe for coloured clothes?
▼

Yes! Smart Wash Original and Lavender Fresh are colour-safe. They clean effectively without fading or bleeding colours.

How do I become a Smart Wash distributor?
▼

We're always looking for new distribution partners. Email your details to recruitment@makotidetergents.co.zw or contact us via WhatsApp to discuss opportunities.

What is the Smart Wash return policy?
▼

We stand behind our products. If you're not satisfied, contact us within 30 days of purchase for a full replacement or refund.
