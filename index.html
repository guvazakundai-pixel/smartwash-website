How To Use

Simple Steps. Brilliant Results.

Just one scoop of Smart Wash is all you need. Follow these 4 easy steps.

1

Measure

Measure the right amount of Smart Wash powder based on load size. One scoop does the job.

2

Agitate

Add powder to water and agitate to fully dissolve. Watch the rich foam build!

3

Soak

Soak clothes for 15–20 minutes. The foam penetrates deep, loosening dirt and stains.

4

Enjoy

Rinse and enjoy the magic! Brilliantly clean clothes with long-lasting fragrance.
