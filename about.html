Who We Are

Proudly Zimbabwean

Smart Wash is manufactured by Makoti Detergents, a proudly Zimbabwean company based in Harare. We specialise in producing high-quality washing powder, dishwashing liquid, pine gel, thick bleach, and fabric softener.

Our products are formulated specifically for Zimbabwean conditions — hard water, tough stains, and heavy use. Every scoop of Smart Wash delivers powerful foam that penetrates deep into fibres, lifting dirt and leaving clothes fresh and clean.

We believe that clean homes lead to healthier, happier communities. That's why we never compromise on quality, even while keeping our prices accessible to every household.
